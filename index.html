About Me

I graduated from UCSD with a Bachelor's in Mathematics–Computer Science, where I built a strong foundation in analytical thinking, problem-solving, and technical skills. During my time there, I gained hands-on experience as a web developer for a student organization and volunteered as a math and English tutor for Burmese refugees.

After graduating, I joined Molina Healthcare as a Senior Associate, where my work focused heavily on technology and data analysis. I specialized in processing and analyzing large datasets, improving data extraction processes, and delivering accurate, timely data to stakeholders. I collaborated with cross-functional teams to identify and resolve data issues, developed SQL-based solutions, and was recognized with an award for outstanding client engagement.

Now, I’m focusing my career on data analytics — leveraging my technical expertise to transform complex datasets into meaningful insights that drive informed business decisions.

Skills: Python, SQL, Data Visualization (Power BI), JavaScript, Java
